Bangkok & Hua Hin, with a two-year-old

Nothing here is booked yet — this page lays out every flight, night-split, and hotel option side by side so you and Salina can pick before we lock a single day-by-day plan. Current thinking: 6 nights, Nepal Airlines out and Thai Airways back, straight to Hua Hin from the airport, Bangkok last.

From Kathmandu (KTM) To Bangkok (BKK) + Hua Hin / Cha-Am Late Nov 2026 Party: 2 adults + 1 toddler (turns 2 on Dec 1)
Best case, all things considered

Nepal Airlines out, Thai Airways back · Mon 23 → Sun 29 Nov · 6 nights

Fly Nepal Airlines to Bangkok (lands 15:45, afternoon light) so the Hua Hin transfer arrives around 19:00 instead of 21:30 — a real bedtime, not a toddler-meltdown one. Fly Thai Airways back on whichever day suits, since it's daily. Mixing carriers gets you the cheaper, better-timed outbound leg and full return-date flexibility in one booking.

Arrives Hua Hin ~19:00, not ~21:30 6 full nights Return date fully flexible Cheaper outbound leg

Two one-way tickets instead of a round-trip fare — usually a bit more than a single round-trip ticket, but the earlier arrival is worth it with a toddler. If you'd rather keep one airline for the whole trip, the all–Thai Airways 6-night version is right below.

The Bangkok animal day, in detail

One flag first: Dusit Zoo (Khao Din) — the old central Bangkok zoo — closed permanently back in 2018 and hasn't reopened; its planned replacement in Pathum Thani isn't open yet either. Don't plan around it. Here's what's actually running.

Safari World & Marine Park — the pick

Drive-through safari plus a marine park with dolphin and sea lion shows. Best full-day option for a toddler if you don't mind the ~1–1.5hr drive out of the city. Closed Mondays — don't schedule this on a Monday.

Sat–Sun hours: 9:00–5:30pm Tue–Fri hours: 9:00–5:00pm Weekend dolphin shows: 1:45pm & 4:15pm Weekend sea lion shows: 11:00am & 2:30pm

Sea Life Bangkok Ocean World — the fallback

Basement of Siam Paragon, central, zero driving, fully air-conditioned. Much shorter visit (allow ~90 min–2hrs) — good if the weather's bad or the toddler's had enough of the sun by then.

Open daily 10:00am–8:00pm Last entry 7:00pm

Since the recommended plan puts this on Saturday 28th, Safari World's weekend hours and both shows are available — no Monday conflict.

Apps to install before you go

Set these up in Kathmandu, not at the Bangkok arrivals hall — some need a working local number or a few minutes to activate.

🚗

Grab

Rides, food delivery, and the BKK↔Hua Hin transfer booking. Link a card before landing.

📶

Airalo or Klook eSIM

Buy a Thailand eSIM and install it before departure — activates the moment you land, no airport SIM queue with a toddler in tow.

🗺️

Google Maps

Download offline maps for Bangkok and Hua Hin/Cha-Am in advance, in case signal is patchy on the drive.

💬

Google Translate

Download the Thai offline pack; the camera translate feature is genuinely useful for menus and signage.

🏨

Booking.com or Agoda

For the actual hotel booking and for managing/cancelling if plans shift.

💱

XE Currency

Quick NPR/THB conversion for on-the-spot decisions at markets and restaurants.

🎟️

Klook

Skip-the-line tickets for Safari World, Sea Life, or the water park — often cheaper than gate price too.

🌦️

Windy or a local weather app

Late Nov is early dry season in Thailand, but a rain check the morning of the zoo day is worth 30 seconds.

Prep checklist, with timing

What to do and by when, working backward from Mon 23 Nov departure.

Now – 6 weeks out

Lock the big decisions

  • Book the Thai Airways flights (Mon 23 → Sun 29 Nov)
  • Book both hotels — Bangkok (1–2 nights) and Hua Hin (4–5 nights)
  • Confirm Thailand entry requirements for Nepali passports (visa or e-visa lead time varies — check early)
  • Buy travel insurance covering the toddler
3–4 weeks out

Sort the logistics

  • Buy and install the Thailand eSIM (activate only once you land)
  • Book Klook tickets for Safari World (or Sea Life) for Sat 28 Nov
  • Decide on the travel car seat for the BKK↔Hua Hin transfer and buy/pack it
  • Request crib/cot at both hotels by email or via the booking site
1–2 weeks out

Confirm and pre-book

  • Pre-book the Grab/private transfer for arrival night (Mon 23, evening) so you're not searching for a ride at 9pm with a tired toddler
  • Reconfirm both hotel reservations and flight times
  • Pack list first pass — passports, meds, sunscreen, diapers, chargers
  • Refill/pack any regular medication with a few days' buffer
Day before (Sun 22)

Final checks

  • Online check-in for the Thai Airways flight if available
  • Charge all devices, download offline Google Maps for Bangkok + Hua Hin
  • Pack the cabin bag: snacks, a change of clothes, diapers, one comfort toy
  • Print or screenshot both hotel confirmations and the flight e-tickets
Day of (Mon 23)

Travel day

  • Leave for Kathmandu airport with buffer for a toddler's pace through check-in/security (Nepal Airlines RA 401, 11:10 departure)
  • Activate the Thailand eSIM as soon as you land at BKK (~15:45)
  • Book the Grab/private transfer to Hua Hin immediately after immigration/baggage
  • Have a change of clothes and snacks ready for the ~3hr afternoon/evening drive — you'll land in Hua Hin around dinnertime, not bedtime

Toddler travel checklist

Everything to sort before wheels-up, regardless of which flight and split you land on.

Documents & health

  • Passports valid 6+ months, photo backup on phone
  • Thailand entry rules confirmed for Nepali passports
  • Travel insurance covering the toddler
  • Basic kids' med kit: fever reducer, rehydration salts, antihistamine, thermometer

Flights

  • Bassinet/seat type confirmed with the airline
  • Child meal pre-booked if available
  • Stroller gate-check policy confirmed
  • Travel car seat decision made for the BKK ↔ Hua Hin leg

Packing

  • Lightweight stroller + carrier as backup
  • SPF 50 sunscreen, sun hat, UV swim shirt
  • Swim + regular diapers (top-ups easy at 7-Eleven/Tops)
  • Kid-safe insect repellent, familiar snacks, a couple of new small toys

Logistics

  • Grab app installed and linked before landing
  • eSIM or airport SIM sorted on arrival
  • THB cash on hand for markets and small vendors
  • Crib/cot ("baby cot") requested at both hotels in advance
  • 1–2 hour nap buffers built around both transfer days
