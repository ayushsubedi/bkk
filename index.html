If it were Pathumwan Princess instead

Holiday Inn Express is what's booked and stays the default — but here's what would actually change if you swapped to Pathumwan Princess (MBK Center) before the cancellation deadline:

  • Safari World drive gets longer, not shorter — Pathumwan/Siam is more central but further from Safari World's northern location than Soi Soonvijai; figure ~45-60min instead of ~30-45min on Fri 27 Nov.
  • Sea Life Bangkok gets much closer — it's in the basement of Siam Paragon, a short walk from Pathumwan Princess, vs. a Grab ride from Soi Soonvijai. Makes it easier to use as the fallback/wind-down stop after Safari World, or as a standalone half-day if the weather turns.
  • Everyday logistics get easier — MBK mall is attached directly to the hotel (food, diapers, aircon breaks without a Grab), and National Stadium BTS is right there for Siam/Sukhumvit-side errands. Soi Soonvijai has less within walking distance.
  • Pool — both have one; Pathumwan Princess's outdoor pool is generally rated larger/better for a toddler to splash around in than a standard Holiday Inn Express pool.
  • Price — Pathumwan Princess runs a bit higher than the ~THB 3,300/night Holiday Inn Express rate, though still mid-range.
  • Hour-by-hour plan — Thu 26 and Sat 28 transfer timings stay about the same either way; only the Fri 27 Safari World drive time in the hour-by-hour above would shift slightly later.

Net: swap only matters if the mall-downstairs convenience and shorter Sea Life trip outweigh paying more and a slightly longer Safari World drive. Holiday Inn Express remains the plan unless you decide to switch.

Hacks — SIM, transfers, money, toddler logistics

Small decisions that save real time or hassle with a 2-year-old and an overnight flight.

SIM / data

Buy the eSIM before you leave Kathmandu

Airalo's Thailand eSIM (or Klook's) installs in minutes over your home WiFi and only activates once it detects a Thai network — so there's zero risk of burning data early. Landing at 05:10 after an overnight flight is the worst possible time to be queuing at an airport SIM counter with a cranky toddler; skip that queue entirely.

Keep your Nepali SIM active but on airplane mode + WiFi

You'll want it live again the moment you land back in KTM. Just toggle data roaming off so it doesn't try to connect in Thailand and rack up charges.

Transfers & getting around

Pre-book the BKK → Hua Hin transfer, don't wing it at 05:30am

Grab works fine, but a 3-hour fixed-route trip straight off an overnight flight is exactly the case for a pre-booked private transfer (hotel concierge, Airport Thailand transfer services, or Grab's scheduled-ride feature) — guaranteed car, fixed price, no surge pricing at an odd hour, and a driver already waiting with a sign instead of you hunting for a pickup point half-asleep with luggage and a toddler.

Request a car seat or bring a compact one

Grab/taxis in Thailand rarely carry compliant child seats. A lightweight travel seat (Cosco Scenera, Mifold, or similar) packs small and covers both the 3hr Hua Hin transfer and Bangkok Grab rides.

Screenshot everything before you lose signal

Save the Dusit Thani and Holiday Inn Express confirmations, the TG 310/319 e-tickets, and the Grab transfer booking as screenshots or offline PDFs — don't rely on having data at the exact moment you need to show a confirmation.

Money

Carry some cash, but you won't need much

THB cash is still useful for markets and small vendors, but hotels, Grab, and most restaurants in Bangkok/Hua Hin take cards. Withdraw from an ATM after landing rather than exchanging at the airport counter — airport exchange rates are consistently the worst in the country.

Holiday Inn Express is pay-at-check-in

That Agoda booking (THB 6,602.98) is charged on arrival, not pre-paid — keep a card or enough cash on hand for check-in day specifically.

Overnight-flight-specific

Shift the toddler's nap schedule a few days early

TG 310 departs KTM at 00:25 — if your child's bedtime is earlier, a couple of days of a slightly later bedtime beforehand softens the overnight-flight shock.

Pack a change of clothes for everyone in the cabin bag

Landing at 05:10 means arriving at the hotel mid-morning, hours before check-in — a quick change out of overnight-flight clothes at the pool/lobby area makes the waiting stretch much more bearable.
